Privacy Policy — Agentic AI Course

Effective date: October 22, 2026

The Agentic AI Course is an educational product offered by ForAllHR ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information is involved when you use the Agentic AI Course website and mobile app (together, the "Course").

Information we collect

  • Course progress stored on your device. The Course saves your lesson progress, quiz results, and access status locally in your browser or app storage on your own device. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.
  • Purchase information. Enrollment payments are processed by Stripe and PayPal on their own secure pages. We receive confirmation of your purchase and the name and email address you provide at checkout so we can deliver course access. We never see or store your full card number or bank details.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us by email, we keep the message and your email address to respond and provide support.

What the app does not do

  • The app does not contain advertising and does not use advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not share your personal information with third parties, except the payment processors named above as needed to complete your purchase.

How we use information

We use the limited information described above only to deliver the Course, provide access and support, issue certificates, and meet legal and accounting obligations.

Data retention and your choices

You can clear your locally stored course progress at any time by clearing the app's or browser's site data. To ask us to delete purchase-related contact information or to ask any privacy question, contact us at the email below. Some purchase records must be retained for tax and accounting purposes.

Children
